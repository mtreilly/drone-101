# Complexity that belongs to the course

The course gradually introduces constraints: ideal linear behaviour, time delay, saturation, noise, motor lag, filtered derivative and page contact. Their flags are experimental variables, not by themselves evidence of a wrong abstraction. A contact must enter the same trajectory that drives plot/readout; precomputed replay must measure the ceiling and recompute if the trajectory changes. Geometry can move with scroll, fonts and prediction gates. Removing observation would simplify implementation by making the lesson false.

The unavoidable boundary is physical state versus page geometry. Shared page-physics chooses solid geometry; chapters choose the teaching response. `watchCeiling` debounces measurements and preserves unchanged replays. Per-lesson adapters and reduced-motion behaviour remain necessary. Accidental retained observers can still be removed without simplifying the physics.

Ten original translations, colour markers, mathematical placeholders and RTL/CJK typography change together with source prose. Bidi isolation must preserve entire expressions; canvas cannot inherit live font metrics automatically. Locale validation plus numerical chapter tests protects necessary semantic consistency. Font rules can be loaded per script, but dropping script coverage, skipping fonts-ready canvas redraw or weakening the validator would not be a valid simplification.

Considered and rejected: universal widget orchestration, globalising all chapter helpers, and hiding physical contacts behind visual-only animation. These reduce visible code at the expense of product truth.
